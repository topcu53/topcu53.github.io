Nova — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026

Nova (“the app”) is a mobile application offering pregnancy and baby tracking. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Important: Nova is not a medical device or healthcare provider. Information in the app is for informational purposes only; always consult your doctor for health decisions.

1. Data Controller

The developer of the Nova app is the data controller.
For privacy requests: btopcu14@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect

  • Account details: email address, password (securely handled by Supabase Auth), preferred language.
  • Profile: your name, parent role (mother/father/caregiver).
  • Child profiles: baby or pregnancy name, gender, estimated due date, birth date, birth story notes, and measurements.
  • Health and care logs: pregnancy tracking (weight, appointments, ultrasounds, etc.), baby care logs, growth, vaccinations, medication reminders, illness diary, teething, milestones.
  • Media: photos and videos you upload (memories, ultrasounds, profile picture).
  • Family access: shared access codes and caregiver session data.
  • Community: posts and comments you share (if you use this feature).
  • Premium: subscription status (via RevenueCat; payment details are processed directly by Apple or Google).
  • Device and usage: notification permissions, app preferences (theme, reminder times); advertising ID on the free plan (Google AdMob).

3. Why We Use Data

  • To create your account and allow you to log in
  • To provide pregnancy and baby tracking features
  • To send reminder notifications (when permitted)
  • To manage Premium subscriptions and family access
  • To display ads on the free plan
  • To improve the app and troubleshoot issues

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and file storage
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • Google AdMob — advertising (Premium users do not see ads)
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — app distribution and in-app purchases

5. Data Retention and Security

Your data is transmitted via encrypted connection (HTTPS) and stored securely within the Supabase infrastructure linked to your account. Access is restricted using Row Level Security (RLS); only you and your authorized caregivers can access your data.

6. Your Rights and Account Deletion Request

We prioritize the privacy and control of our users' data. You can use one of the two methods below to delete your account and all associated data:

1. Deletion from Within the App:

  • Log in to the app.
  • Go to the Settings menu.
  • Tap on "Delete Account" and confirm.
  • This process instantly and permanently deletes your profile details, child records, and all data from our servers (Supabase).

2. Deletion Request via Email:

  • Send us an email at btopcu14@gmail.com using the email address registered to your account.
  • Write "Account Deletion Request - Nova" in the subject line.
  • Once we receive your request, your data will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.

Data Retention: When your account is deleted, all personal data (media files, growth records, community posts, etc.) is irreversibly removed from our databases (Supabase). (Except for billing/subscription records that are legally required to be retained).
